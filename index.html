FAQ

Questions Parents Often Ask

Can't find what you're looking for? Our admissions team is always happy to chat.

📞 Speak to Admissions
💬 WhatsApp Us Instead →

What age does Ignis School start from?

+

We welcome children from age 3 in our Kindergarten programme, all the way through to Primary 6 (age 12). We believe in starting early to build a strong foundation.

What is the school's academic calendar?

+

Ignis School follows the Ghana Education Service academic calendar, with three terms per year. School hours are typically 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM, with after-school options available.

Are the teachers qualified and trained?

+

Yes. All our teachers hold professional teaching qualifications and undergo regular professional development training. We believe a school is only as strong as its educators.

Is there a school uniform?

+

Yes, Ignis School has an official uniform. Details, sizes, and where to purchase are provided at the time of enrolment. The uniform is affordable and readily available.

Do you offer school transportation?

+

We are working to provide transport arrangements for certain routes. Please contact the admissions office to discuss your location and available options.

How do I visit the school?

+

Simply call us on 030 291 3951 or fill in the enquiry form above to schedule a visit. We welcome parents to come see the school, meet our staff, and ask all their questions.
